02 / WHY WORK WITH ME

UGC instincts.
More product context.

The goal is not to look like an actor reading a brand brief. It is to understand what matters about the product, find the angle, and deliver it like a person.

01

On-camera delivery

Direct-to-camera, interview-style, demonstrations, voiceover, and conversational delivery that can fit the product instead of forcing one persona onto everything.

02

Hook + concept development

I think beyond the first line. The hook, scenario, proof, B-roll, and payoff should all support the same reason the viewer keeps watching.

03

Technical fluency

Apps, AI tools, SaaS, workflows, and technical products are comfortable territory, which makes it easier to turn features into human outcomes.

04

Format flexibility

One product can become a demo, problem-solution video, interview, POV, or testimonial without forcing every idea into the same script.
